Our Story

Kambingan Eatery started as a small family-run stall along the highway, serving hearty goat dishes to locals and travelers alike. Over the years, it has grown into a favorite neighborhood spot — known for its generous portions, honest flavors, and friendly service.

We take pride in serving food that feels like home — slow-cooked, fresh, and made with care. Every customer is family.

Today, we continue that tradition. From our kitchen to your table, we make sure every dish is prepared with the same love and attention as the first day we opened.

Family-run since day one
